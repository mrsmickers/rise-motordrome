African Grand Prix

Formula 1 signed a 10-year deal with Rwanda on 13 October 2026. The first race is due in 2030 at the earliest. This circuit follows the October 2026 render and fan reconstructions of it.

Assists

  • Beginner: racing line chevrons, steering follows the line (your steering moves you across it), braking help, traction control, auto recovery
  • Intermediate: chevrons in the braking zones only, traction control, help at the track edge, auto recovery
  • Advanced: no help
  • Chevrons go yellow when you need to lift and red when you need to brake

Controls

  • W↑ throttle
  • S↓Space brake
  • AD steer
  • Shift boost
  • V camera, 1–6 pick one
  • P hot lap autopilot
  • R back on track
  • T time of day
  • C reverse direction
  • L assist level
  • N sound, H HUD, F full screen
  • Esc back to the menu
  • Drag to look around, scroll to zoom
